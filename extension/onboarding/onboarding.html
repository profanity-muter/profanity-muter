Profanity Muter

Set up profanity filtering for YouTube™ in about two minutes.

How it works

  • The audio is transcribed on your device. A speech model runs inside your browser and reads the audio of the video you're watching. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is sent to us, and no account is involved - there is no server in this picture at all.
  • Matched words are silenced as they play. When a word from the active list is found, the video is muted for exactly that moment and unmuted straight after. The word is removed, not covered over - nothing is added to the audio.
  • Captions are censored too. If subtitles or the transcript panel are open, matched words are replaced there as well, so the word isn't simply read instead of heard.

What it won't do

Profanity Muter reduces profanity. It does not eliminate it.

  • Analysis trails the video. Transcribing takes a moment, so the start of a video - and the seconds right after you skip around - may not be analyzed yet. That window is the most likely place for a word to slip through. The next step lets you decide what should happen during it.
  • Speech recognition is imperfect. Music, crosstalk, accents, mumbling and noise all cause missed or mis-heard words. A word can be missed. A clean word can occasionally be muted by mistake.
  • It only knows the words on the list. Slang, misspellings and new coinages won't match until someone adds them.
  • English only. It filters English speech. Other languages are not muted yet.
  • Caption censoring depends on the captions. If a video has no subtitles, or its subtitles are poorly timed or leave words out, there is nothing on screen for us to censor - the audio filtering still runs either way.
  • Regular YouTube videos only. Shorts, livestreams and premieres aren't supported, and nothing outside youtube.com is filtered: other sites and apps play exactly as they always have. On a Short you'll see a note saying so rather than a filter that quietly isn't running.
  • YouTube changes can break it. The filter depends on how YouTube's player works today. When YouTube changes something, filtering can stop until we ship a fix. If that happens, the extension will tell you rather than stay quiet about it.

No automated filter is perfect, and this one isn't either. Treat it as a good filter rather than a guarantee - the same way you'd treat a spam folder.

Parental lock (optional)

Set a password to stop these settings being changed.

A password is set. You can remove it from the toolbar popup.

This is a deterrent, not security: anyone who can open chrome://extensions can clear this extension's storage or remove it. There is no way to reset or recover the password: it is stored only on your device and we keep no backup of it. If you forget it, the only option is to remove and re-add the extension, which also clears your settings.
